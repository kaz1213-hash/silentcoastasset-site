FAILURE → RESEARCH → REBUILD / FX UNIVERSITY

He tried to rebuild one trading system. He accidentally built a university.

An eye clinic director who cannot write a single line of code asks ChatGPT to rebuild an automated FX strategy from a decade ago. A long backtest then reveals roughly 14.9 years underwater. What follows is a nonfiction chronicle of failure, research, backtesting, AI-assisted rebuilds—and six algorithmic “professors.”

WHY THIS BOOK IS DIFFERENT

Not another “let AI make money for you” book.

Promising ideas break under longer tests. Professor candidates get rejected. Failed systems go to a graveyard instead of disappearing. The rules get rewritten. ALGORITHMIC FX TRADING, WITHOUT THE VICTORY LAP

深さだけでなく、時間を見る。

長期バックテストでQuickShiftのMTMは、2011年9月の高値から2026年7月末まで新高値を更新できませんでした。約5,426日、14.9年。Drawdownは「どれだけ沈んだか」、Underwaterは「どれだけ長く戻れなかったか」。この違いが、一本のEAを復活させる話を、ポートフォリオと制度設計の話へ変えていきます。

ALGORITHMIC FX TRADING, WITHOUT THE VICTORY LAP

Drawdown is depth. Underwater is time.

In the long-run research simulation, QuickShift's MTM equity failed to make a new high from September 2011 through the end of July 2026: roughly 5,426 days, or 14.9 years. That distinction between depth and recovery time changes the project from “revive one EA” into a story about portfolio design, strategy validation and survival.
