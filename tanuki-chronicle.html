ENGLISH EDITORIAL PRINCIPLE

Translate the facts. Re-edit the story. Preserve the person.

Do not polish away the weirdness.

English v1.0 is not a literal translation layer. It is designed to read naturally in English without sanding away the source's humor, odd details, contradictions, concrete stakes, or cultural texture.

HOW IT WORKS

From archive to Chronicle.

1. Open the reader

Open the included Tanuki Chronicle HTML reader on your computer.

2. Select your archive

Choose a Project Vault ZIP or supported ChatGPT/OpenAI archive ZIP.

3. Build the RUN ZIP

The reader prepares a Chronicle RUN package locally.

4. Use your own ChatGPT

You upload the RUN ZIP to your own ChatGPT and follow the included launch handoff to generate the Chronicle.

PRIVACY

Clear about what stays local — and what does not.

The included Chronicle reader does not automatically upload your selected archive to Silent Coast Asset. It creates the RUN ZIP locally. The Chronicle generation step happens after you manually upload that RUN ZIP to your own ChatGPT. Tanuki Chronicle is therefore not described as “fully local AI,” and we do not claim that your data never leaves your computer.
